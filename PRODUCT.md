# Product

> Default context for this prototype. Rewrite it (by hand or via `$impeccable teach`) once the prototype's real product is known — until then, these defaults apply.

## What this is

A prototype. The product being designed is whatever the designer describes — treat each screen as part of one coherent, plausible product unless told otherwise.

## Audience

Whoever the designer says. Default: professional users who value clarity and speed over decoration.

## Tone & personality

Calm, minimal, confident. The interface stays quiet so the content does the talking.

## Anti-references

- Cluttered dashboards that cram every metric above the fold
- Gradient-heavy SaaS landing pages
- Decorative illustrations and icon noise
